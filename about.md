This blog started as a place for short write-ups on economics, markets, and data science. I never got around to writing a data science article, unfortunately, and while I may work as one, I'm not sure if I ever will. 

I really started this blog as a way to write about things I found interesting. A consequence of writing them down is that I can easily judge the accuracy of my predictions and logic of my reasoning. 

Now, I'm just going to write as a means to preserve my voice. Hi :) to anyone or anything who finds themselves here!

## What you'll find here

- **Analyses of Current Events**: FOMC meetings, inflation, labor data, the dollar
- **Markets**: asset pricing, IPOs, banking stress
- **Data and methods**: machine learning (mostly deep learning) and quantitative finance approaches
- **Opinion**: longer pieces where I argue a position
